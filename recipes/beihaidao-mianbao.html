Hokkaido Milk Bread

Desserts · 450g loaf tin

Ingredients (450g loaf tin)

Tangzhong

Bread flour20g
Water100g

Main Dough

Bread flour 250g
(if protein <14%: 25g Vital Wheat Gluten + 225g flour) （protein <14%时：25g Wheat Gluten + 225g面粉）
Sugar30g
Salt3g
Dry yeast4g
Milk (cold)79g
Condensed milk20g
Heavy cream30g
Unsalted butter, softened 20g (used 23g this time) （这次用了23克）
Anthony's
Vital Wheat Gluten

① Tangzhong

1
在锅中加入水和面粉。 Add water and flour to a small saucepan.
2
开小火加热并不断搅拌。 Heat over low flame, stirring constantly.
3
当面糊逐渐变黏稠，出现明显的纹路。 Continue until the paste thickens and leaves visible lines when stirred.
4
温度达到65°C即可关火。（其实粘稠了就基本到65°C了。）盖保鲜膜，用冰水混合物在外面快速冷却；不着急用的话室温慢慢冷却也可以。 Remove from heat at 65°C — it's basically there once it's thick. Cover with plastic wrap and cool in an ice bath, or let cool at room temperature if not in a hurry.

② Main Dough

1
在搅拌盆中加入高筋面粉、细砂糖、盐粗略搅拌，然后加入干酵母搅拌均匀。这样可以防止酵母与盐直接接触影响活性。 Combine bread flour, sugar, and salt in the mixing bowl with a brief stir, then add dry yeast and mix. This prevents the yeast and salt from coming into direct contact.
2
将牛奶、淡奶油、炼乳混合均匀。 Whisk together the cold milk, heavy cream, and condensed milk.
3
将汤种与液体一起加入搅拌盆中。 Add the tangzhong and the liquid mixture to the flour bowl.
4
用刮刀搅拌成面块，这样可以加快揉面的速度。 Use a spatula to bring everything together into a rough dough — this speeds up kneading.
5
开启 KitchenAid stir 档揉面，2分钟成团，不用光滑，静置15分钟。不成团很黏的话是面粉protein需要时间吸水，或面粉protein含量不够。（家里如果热，盆底放冰水混合物的盆，静置时不要让面团变热。） KitchenAid on Stir — 2 min until it just comes together (doesn't need to be smooth), then rest 15 min. If still very sticky, the flour needs more time to hydrate or has insufficient protein. If kitchen is warm, set the bowl over an ice bath during the rest.
6
KitchenAid 开 Stir 或 2 档，揉面约20分钟。取一小块面团，能拉出较薄的膜，破裂口有少量锯齿，此时面团8成筋度。 KitchenAid on Stir or Speed 2, ~20 minutes. Test: stretch a small piece — it should form a thin film that tears with a slightly jagged edge. This is 80% gluten development.
7
加入软化的黄油。 Add the softened butter.
8
先用刮刀搅拌几下，方便后面混合均匀。要避免黄油融化，否则不易融入面团。 Use a spatula to roughly incorporate the butter before turning the machine on. Avoid letting the butter melt — it becomes harder to absorb into the dough.
9
用厨师机低速将面团与黄油揉至完全混合均匀，大约2分钟。 KitchenAid on low speed until butter is fully incorporated, about 2 minutes.
10
转为中速揉面，揉到面团随搅拌钩猛烈拍打盆壁的状态，面团表面会变得非常光滑。 Increase to medium speed. Knead until the dough slaps the bowl vigorously and the surface is very smooth.
11
能拉出均匀的薄膜，破裂口很圆滑，此时面团10成筋度。这个过程大约用了3分钟。 Windowpane test: stretch a small piece — it should form a translucent, even film that tears with a smooth round hole. This is full gluten development (100%). Took about 3 minutes.
12
此时面团温度为26°C。 Dough temperature at this point: 26°C (79°F).
13
将面团揉圆放入抹油的盆中。 Shape the dough into a ball and place in a lightly oiled bowl.
14
盖上保鲜膜进行一次发酵。室温低于25°C则需借助烤箱发酵；烤箱没有proof功能的话，烧点水倒烤盘里放进去。冬天基本需要烤箱发酵。 Cover with plastic wrap and proof. If room temperature is below 25°C, proof in the oven (use a pan of hot water if no proof setting). In winter, oven proofing is almost always needed.
15
当面团发酵至体积变为2倍大，用手指插入不回弹不塌陷，即说明发酵到位。塌陷则发酵过度，回弹则发酵不足。约90分钟。可以用矿泉水瓶盖法观察两倍大。 First proof is done when the dough doubles in size and a finger poke holds without springing back or collapsing. Collapses = over-proofed; springs back = under-proofed. About 90 minutes. Tip: use a water bottle cap to track the starting volume.

③ Shaping

1
将面团取出，用手掌从中间向四周均匀按压排气。 Turn the dough out and press from the centre outward with your palm to degas evenly.
2
进行一次三折，这一步能让吐司的组织更加细腻，也能促进二次发酵。 Fold the dough in thirds (like a letter). This refines the crumb and helps the second proof.
3
将面团均匀分割成三等份，每份约180g。分割尽量一步到位，避免产生太多小面团，不利于保持面筋。 Divide into 3 equal pieces, ~180g each. Try to cut cleanly in one motion — too many scraps weaken the gluten structure.
4
将三个面团分别揉圆。 Shape each piece into a smooth ball.
5
用保鲜膜盖好防止表面风干，静置15分钟。静置是为了让面团松弛，方便下一步的擀卷。 Cover with plastic wrap and bench rest 15 minutes. This relaxes the gluten so the dough rolls out easily.
6
将静置好的面团擀压成面饼形，翻转光滑面朝下。 Roll each ball into a flat oval, then flip it smooth-side down.
7
左右各折入三分之一。 Fold the left and right thirds into the centre.
8
再次擀压成长条形状，面团底边拉宽压薄。 Roll out again into a long strip, pressing the far edge thin and wide.
9
按压掉侧边的气泡。 Press out any air bubbles along the sides.
10
用双手将面团卷起，要卷紧一点，大约2–3圈。 Roll up the dough tightly with both hands, about 2–3 turns.
11
卷的圈数多则组织会更细腻，但不是越多越好。 More rolls = finer crumb, but more is not always better.
12
将卷好的面团按同一旋转方向放入吐司盒内。 Place all three rolls in the loaf tin with the seam facing the same direction.
13
放入烤箱进行二次发酵。放点水增加湿度，冬天用开水。 Place in oven for second proof with a pan of water for humidity — use boiling water in winter.

④ Baking

14
当面团发酵到接近模具顶部，高度约9成，用手指按压缓慢回弹，则说明发酵到位。夏天约90分钟，冬天用烤箱proof约2小时。 Second proof is done when the dough reaches ~90% of the tin height and a finger poke springs back slowly. Summer: ~90 min. Winter: ~2 hours in oven.
15
盖上盖子，送入烤箱底层。 Cover with the lid and place on the bottom rack of the oven.
16
我是盖着锡箔纸175°C（347°F）烤35分钟，然后用broil上色。也可以刷糖水上色。之后用有盖子的吐司盒就不用管上色了。 175°C (347°F) covered with foil for 35 min, then broil to brown the top. Alternatively brush with sugar water. If using a lidded Pullman pan, skip the browning step.
要根据烤箱温差灵活调整。 / Adjust temperature based on your oven's actual heat.
