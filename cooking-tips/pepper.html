Pepper

Black vs white pepper — when to use each.

Black and white pepper come from the same plant but taste very different. Black pepper is aromatic and enhances fragrance; white pepper is sharper and adds heat. Knowing which to reach for makes a real difference.

Black vs White

Black Pepper

黑胡椒
  • Enhances aroma (提香) — adds a warm, fragrant depth
  • Made from unripe berries, dried with skin on
  • Flavor: complex, woody, slightly floral
  • Best used in marinades, stir-fries, pasta, grilled meats

White Pepper

白胡椒
  • Has spicy heat (有辣味) — sharper, more pungent
  • Made from ripe berries with skin removed
  • Flavor: clean, hot, earthy
  • Best used in soups, light-colored sauces, Chinese stir-fries, dumplings

When to Use Which

Dish / Use Pepper Why
Beef steak / grilled meats Black Aromatic crust and depth
Chinese soups (e.g. hot & sour, egg drop) White Heat without dark specks; clean flavor
Dumpling filling (猪肉馅) White Traditional; removes gaminess
Pasta (cacio e pepe, carbonara) Black Core flavor; visible pepper is part of the dish
Light cream sauces / white fish White Keeps sauce color clean; subtle heat
Stir-fried vegetables Either Black for fragrance; white for a clean spicy kick
