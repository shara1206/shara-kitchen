Where to Buy Free-Range Chicken

Seattle / Eastside buying guide

Channels for buying free-range / pasture-raised chicken around Seattle, collected from community comment threads — supermarkets, a seafood shop, a deli, and group-buy sellers.

Channels & Community Feedback

Type Name / Channel Community Feedback
Supermarket T&T Supermarket (大统华) Sells pre-cut free-range chicken; plain-boiled it still tastes clearly of chicken. Cantonese shoppers vouch for it — good for coconut chicken hotpot.
Supermarket PCC Carries pasture-raised birds; ask the meat counter to cut it up for you.
Supermarket Whole Foods Free-range birds with no off/gamey smell; non-frozen “Better Chicken Project” option available.
Supermarket H Mart (Redmond) Fresh pre-chopped Longgang chicken; commenters rate it the most chicken-flavored of the bunch.
Seafood shop Xiongda Seafood (熊大海鲜) Takes advance orders for fresh chicken and fresh duck.
Deli Dongjiang BBQ, Chinatown (东江烧腊店) Roast chicken, roast duck and braised meats are all good — cooked food, not raw ingredients.
Group buy / site Toofull Seattle The “大雨” mentioned in comments actually refers to this site / group-buy channel. Carries sanhuang chicken.
Group buy Runxi | He (润汐｜和) Shoppers say her chicken is tasty and fresh, but you have to chop it yourself.

Source: community comment threads — not verified in person. Stock and sellers change; call ahead for group-buy and pre-order channels.

Quick Takeaways

  • Want it pre-chopped? T&T and H Mart Redmond both sell chicken already cut up — the least work.
  • Want the strongest chicken flavor? Commenters rank H Mart Redmond's fresh Longgang chicken first, T&T's free-range second.
  • Want no gamey smell? Whole Foods free-range, especially the non-frozen "Better Chicken Project" birds.
  • Want true pasture-raised? PCC — buy the whole bird and have the meat counter cut it.
  • Freshest possible: pre-order from Xiongda Seafood, or group-buy sellers (Toofull sanhuang chicken, Runxi | He) — but expect to chop it yourself.
  • Not cooking at all? Dongjiang BBQ in Chinatown for roast chicken, roast duck, and braised meats.
