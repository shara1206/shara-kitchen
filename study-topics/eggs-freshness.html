      What the age means

      Age State Best for
      0–7 days Peak Poaching, sunny-side-up, tamagoyaki — the white still holds together. Too fresh to peel well hard-boiled.
      8–21 days Prime Anything. Hard-boiled eggs finally peel cleanly from about day 10.
      22–35 days Fine, cooked Baking, scrambling, custards. The white spreads thin in a pan.
      Over 35 days Check it Float test: a fresh egg lies flat in water, an old one stands up or floats. A floater goes in the bin.

      Ignore the printed "sell-by" date — it can be set up to 30 days after packing, so two cartons sharing a sell-by can differ by two weeks in real age.
