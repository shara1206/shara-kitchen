Best Eggs Around Bellevue: Is PCC Mobile Pastured It?

Local buying decision · Sept 20, 2026

TL;DR

PCC Mobile Pastured is the best supermarket egg around Bellevue by a wide margin — but it is not the best egg available to me. Buy it as the default staple; buy farmers-market eggs when the egg is the dish.

Where it actually ranks

PCC's own private-label Mobile Pastured line is one of only about 31 egg lines nationwide with the Cornucopia Institute's top 5-egg rating. The numbers behind that rating are genuinely strong, not marketing:

What the 5-egg rating is actually rewarding

★★★★★
  • The coops move. Birds get fresh forage on rotation instead of a bare, permanently trampled dirt yard — this is the single biggest difference from brands that merely hold the "pasture-raised" certification.
  • No beak trimming. Allowed under most organic certifications; not done here.
  • Stacked certifications: Certified Humane + Non-GMO Project + Salmon Safe, plus on-farm manure composting and regenerative practices.
  • Transparency. Cornucopia noted the supplier was openly cooperative during its investigation — most 1–2 egg brands were not.

The honest caveats

Two things the rating doesn't capture

  • 1. It's a contract operation, not a small farm. The supplier (Wilcox-affiliated) runs mostly conventional production on the same property — organic/pastured is the minority line. Cornucopia flagged this explicitly. Not disqualifying, but it is not the mental image of a small family farm the carton suggests.
  • 2. Freshness is the real gap. Retail eggs are typically 1–3 weeks old at shelf; direct-from-farm eggs are days old. That difference is far more visible in a poached egg, a custard, or a plain tamagoyaki than any welfare score is.

The other 5-egg Washington farms

All Cornucopia 5-egg rated and smaller than PCC's supplier. The first three are stocked at PCC, so they need no extra trip:

  • Misty Meadows Farm — Everson, WA (~100 mi north). Sold at PCC.
  • Windy N Ranch — Ellensburg, WA (~100 mi east). Sold at PCC; also ships direct.
  • Skokomish Valley Farms — Shelton, WA (~65 mi southwest). Smallest of the group.
  • Skagit River Ranch — Sedro-Woolley, WA. Farmers-market regular in Seattle; best known for grass-fed beef.

The practical call

  • Default staple: PCC Mobile Pastured. Best welfare-per-dollar on any shelf I can reach on a normal errand.
  • When the egg is the dish (poached, custard, tamagoyaki, egg-drop soup): farmers-market or farm-direct eggs, for age not ethics.
  • Easy middle path: Misty Meadows or Windy N, both sold at PCC alongside the private label — same 5-egg tier, smaller operation, no extra trip.
  • Still skip: generic store-brand "organic" eggs (Kirkland, 365, Simple Truth, Trader Joe's) — see the brand comparison note; they rate 1–2 eggs.

Correction to an earlier assumption: Vital Farms is not a lower-tier brand — it also holds a Cornucopia 5-egg rating and is listed in the top tier of the brand comparison note. The national brand worth being skeptical of is the generic store-label "organic" carton, not Vital Farms.
