Organic Milk Brand Comparison

Source: The Cornucopia Institute Dairy Scorecard · June 8, 2026

TL;DR

Same trusted rater as the egg scorecard: The Cornucopia Institute rates 160+ organic dairy brands on a 0–5 cow scale. Same pattern as eggs: small family farms with real pasture rate highest; the biggest "organic" brands often rate worst because they're industrial-scale CAFO operations that meet the letter (not the spirit) of organic. Notably, Horizon Organic — one of the biggest names — gets a 0 (refused to participate).

Cornucopia's dairy rating scale

Notable brand: Alexandre Family Farm — buy the grassfed line

Alexandre Family Farm 100% Grassfed

★★☆☆☆

Cornucopia score: 310 / 1100 — 2-cow “Fair” rating. National. Products: fluid milk, yogurt, A2, 100% grassfed.

Why so low despite impressive certifications? Alexandre holds Regenerative Organic Certified, Certified Humane, OPT Certified Grassfed, and Land to Market Verified Regenerative labels — an impressive stack on paper. However, Cornucopia placed this brand “under increased scrutiny” after confirmed animal abuse and neglect on supplier farms, plus documented non-compliances with USDA National Organic Program standards. Multiple scorecard categories (animal welfare, pasture, organic commitment) were penalized under Cornucopia’s adjustment policy, pulling the score from what would otherwise be a high rating down to 2 cows. The operation did correct the non-compliances per the NOP investigation.

Key distinction — the two lines are different: The compliance issues (animal abuse, NOP non-compliances) were documented on supplier farms for their other product lines. The 100% Grassfed line sources exclusively from Alexandre-owned farms, not outside suppliers. Cornucopia even scored the grassfed line’s pasture management highly (75 pts) before penalties were applied.

Grassfed line quality: OPT Certified Grassfed, 60%+ DMI from grazing, rotational grazing with year-round vegetative cover, milked 2×/day, calves in group housing. Nutritionally this is a high-quality grassfed milk with a strong omega-3 and CLA profile. This is the line I buy.

On the low Cornucopia score: The 2-cow rating reflects brand-level penalties applied across all lines due to the compliance history. It does not reflect the grassfed line’s actual production quality. Worth re-checking in a year to see if Cornucopia lifts the scrutiny flag.

Avoid (0–2 cows)

Industrial organic or non-transparent

★☆☆☆☆

Big industrial operations or brands that refused Cornucopia's investigator access. The "organic" label is technically valid but minimal pasture and oversight.

Horizon Organic Aurora Organic Dairy Applegate organic dairy Costco Kirkland Signature Organic (OEM by Aurora) Many supermarket organic private labels

The Aurora problem: Aurora Organic Dairy is a massive industrial-scale "organic" supplier that produces private-label organic milk for Walmart, Costco, Target, Wegmans, Trader Joe's, and many regional chains. Cornucopia has documented repeated organic violations on Aurora farms. If a store-brand organic milk is suspiciously cheap, it's likely Aurora.

How to use this

  • Default choice: Organic Valley — widely available, 4-cow rating, decent price.
  • Best fat profile: Maple Hill or Organic Valley Grassmilk — 100% grass-fed.
  • Skip: Horizon Organic. Despite the name, it's rated 0 cows.
  • Check store brand suppliers: If "Distributed by..." mentions Aurora or Platteville CO, it's the industrial source.
  • Full scorecard: cornucopia.org/scorecard/dairy
